ABOUT ME

🐼 💖 🌸

Hi my name is Krystal Alexandra Veguilla. I'm from Indianapolis, Indiana. I went to Cathedral High School. I'm currently a Political Science and Sociology major, and eventually I want to add a Criminology and Criminal Justice minor. But I am on a pre-law track to become either a family or immigration lawyer. My favorite color is pink, like almost everything I own is pink. Some of my hobbies include cooking, baking, reading, and watching tv. Here at IU, I really want to join a multicultural sorority and the Puerto Rican Student Organization Club. Some other clubs I would join are associated with La Casa Cultural House here on campus. I am very proud of being half Puerto Rican and half Peruvian, so I'm really excited to find my community of fellow Latinos. I love animals and helping people. I would say my favorite animal though are pandas. For reading and writing, I love reading as a hobby and I also love writing when it's my free time. I really enjoy romance and fantasy books. So, for class sometimes it's a little harder, but I still very much enjoy it. Anything English or Social Studies has always been my favorite subject growing up.
